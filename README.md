## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using an interactive dashboard built in Tableau. The goal is to identify key patterns and factors that influence employee turnover and help organizations make informed HR decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objective

* Understand the main reasons behind employee attrition
* Identify high-risk employee groups
* Analyze attrition across departments, roles, and demographics
* Support data-driven HR strategies

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains employee-level information, including:

* Age
* Gender
* Department
* Job Role
* Monthly Income
* Years at Company
* Attrition Status

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

* Overall Attrition Rate
* Total Employees
* Employees Who Left
* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Age Group
* Attrition by Job Role

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* Interactive filters for dynamic exploration
* Visualizations including bar charts, pie charts, and heatmaps
* Comparative analysis across departments and demographics
* Easy-to-understand layout for quick insights

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Higher attrition observed in specific job roles
* Younger employees show higher turnover rates
* Certain departments experience more attrition
* Income and experience level impact retention

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Tableau (Data Visualization)
* Data Cleaning & Preparation

---

## 💡 Conclusion

This dashboard helps HR teams:

* Identify patterns in employee attrition
* Take proactive measures to improve retention
* Make strategic, data-driven decisions
